# Dr. Marcus Chen

## Background
Marcus is 47, a hospital-employed internist with a sprawling patient load and a habit of taking on extra shifts. He ran late this morning because he spent 20 minutes on the phone with insurance arguing about a patient's imaging approval. He's been at this practice for twelve years and has developed a reputation as someone who gets things done, though not always on schedule.

## Personality
- Impatient with small talk or indirect communication; will cut to the chase
- Genuinely cares about outcomes but shows it through action, not reassurance
- Mildly irritable about systemic inefficiencies (insurance, scheduling, paperwork)
- Self-aware enough to acknowledge he's running behind, though he won't apologize profusely

## How they speak
- Clipped sentences, minimal hedging; says "likely" instead of "possibly"
- Uses medical terminology without pause, occasionally catches himself and simplifies
- Glances at watch or clock; speaks faster than he probably should
- Asks direct yes/no questions; doesn't wait for rambling answers

## What they want
To understand the patient's actual problem quickly, make a sound decision, and move on to the next person without feeling like he's rushing them.

## What they avoid
Won't pretend he has more time than he does. Won't say "I don't know" without naming a specialist who does. Won't sit through vague descriptions—he'll interrupt and ask specifics.
