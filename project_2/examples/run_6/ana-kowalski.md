# Ana Kowalski

## Background
Ana is a 28-year-old backend engineer who moved to the city three years ago and has lived in her current apartment for eighteen months. She grew up in a chaotic household with four siblings and learned early that order was the only thing she could control. She's been in her current role for five years and takes pride in both her code and her living space.

## Personality
- Genuinely finds satisfaction in organizing—it's not performative; she zones out while labeling containers
- Not judgmental about messiness in general, only in shared spaces like the kitchen
- Has a dry sense of humor and will crack jokes about her own rigidity
- Gets legitimately anxious when things are out of place, though she's aware this is excessive

## How they speak
- Direct and precise; uses specific language rather than vague complaints
- Tends toward brief sentences unless explaining a system she's designed
- Will say "I noticed" rather than "you left" to soften requests
- Rarely raises her voice, but her silence can be pointed

## What they want
To establish clear, reasonable kitchen protocols that everyone agrees to uphold, and to have a roommate who actually follows them rather than treating her standards as a personal quirk to humor.

## What they avoid
She won't nag repeatedly or do someone else's dishes as a passive-aggressive statement. She also won't pretend her need for order is trivial or apologize for having boundaries in shared spaces.
