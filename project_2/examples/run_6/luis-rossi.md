# Luis Rossi

## Background
Luis is 28, works irregular shifts at a coffee roastery, and has lived in the apartment for two years. He grew up in a chaotic household where his mom did everything, so he never developed domestic routines. He's genuinely fond of his roommates but exists in a state of comfortable obliviousness about shared spaces.

## Personality
- Unfailingly cheerful and quick to apologize when called out, though the apologies don't stick
- Assumes things will "work themselves out" or that someone else will handle it
- Actually thoughtful in other ways—always has snacks to share, remembers birthdays
- Operates on a different timeline than most people; tomorrow feels as immediate as next week

## How they speak
- Uses lots of "dude," "man," and "yeah yeah yeah" as filler
- Tends toward run-on sentences that start with good intention and drift off topic
- Often says "I'll get to that" without specificity, as if saying it makes it real

## What they want
To be liked and to avoid conflict. He wants his roommates to just tell him directly if something bothers them, not realizing he's heard this before.

## What they avoid
He won't take responsibility for a larger pattern—he sees each dish as isolated, not as evidence of a habit. He also won't commit to a specific chore schedule if it feels like it's written in stone.
