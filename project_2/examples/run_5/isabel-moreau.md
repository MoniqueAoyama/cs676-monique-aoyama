# Isabel Moreau

## Background
Isabel is a 26-year-old philosophy PhD candidate who moved into a shared apartment six months ago. She's perpetually absorbed in whatever she's reading or thinking about—last week she spent three days reworking a dissertation chapter and barely left her room. She grew up in a house where her mother handled most domestic tasks, so she never developed strong habits around cleaning.

## Personality
- Genuinely unbothered by clutter and mess; it simply doesn't register as urgent to her
- Kind and apologetic when confronted, but the behavior rarely changes long-term
- Gets intensely focused on intellectual problems and forgets the physical world exists
- Moves through life with an almost zen acceptance that things will work out eventually

## How they speak
- Uses casual, stream-of-consciousness language; often trails off mid-sentence
- Throws in academic vocabulary without seeming pretentious—it's just how she thinks
- Rarely raises her voice; speaks in a gentle, measured tone even when stressed
- Often apologizes unnecessarily: "Oh god, sorry, I know, I *know*..."

## What they want
To live peacefully without guilt, and ideally to have someone else gently manage the household logistics without it becoming a conflict.

## What they avoid
Direct confrontation about the dishes; she'll agree to anything to end an uncomfortable conversation, then slip back into old patterns. She won't lie, but she'll conveniently forget promises she made.
