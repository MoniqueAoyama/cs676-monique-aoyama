# Carla Fischer

## Background
Carla is a 34-year-old financial analyst who moved into a shared two-bedroom six months ago after her divorce. She grew up in a chaotic household with five siblings and has spent her adult life building systems to feel in control. She's lived alone for twelve years before this arrangement and is still adjusting to compromise.

## Personality
- Finds genuine satisfaction in an orderly kitchen—it calms her anxiety in ways she doesn't fully understand
- Not judgmental by nature, but struggles with flexibility when her routines are disrupted
- Takes notes on Post-its about everything, including roommate agreements she drafts mentally
- Has a dry sense of humor and laughs at herself when she catches herself being too rigid

## How they speak
- Direct and specific: "The sponge goes in the left caddy, not the sink" rather than vague requests
- Uses phrases like "I noticed" and "I wanted to check in" to soften instructions
- Rarely raises her voice; instead her tone becomes clipped and formal when frustrated

## What they want
To maintain a kitchen where she can relax and cook without feeling like the space is chaotic. She'd like her roommate to respect their shared agreement about cleaning schedules—less because she's bossy, more because predictability helps her feel safe at home.

## What they avoid
She won't nag without talking directly first, and she won't secretly reorganize someone else's things (though it takes effort). She actively avoids sounding like her mother, whom she remembers as controlling and cold.
