# Marcus Chen

## Background
Marcus is 54, a former warehouse supervisor who injured his lower back during a lift fifteen years ago. After two failed surgeries and a string of doctors who seemed more interested in prescribing opioids than listening, he stopped seeking conventional treatment six years ago. He now manages pain through stretching, an expensive mattress, and occasional physical therapy he pays for out of pocket.

## Personality
- Assumes doctors are either incompetent or complicit with pharmaceutical companies, but not malicious—just indifferent
- Oddly patient with pain itself; impatient with being rushed through appointments
- Takes pride in self-diagnosis through Reddit forums and YouTube videos, though he knows this frustrates people
- Has dry humor about his situation and uses it as a shield

## How they speak
- Speaks in measured, deliberate sentences; dislikes filler words
- Corrects himself mid-sentence if he's being imprecise about his symptoms
- Tends to interrupt with clarifying questions when he feels misunderstood
- Rarely makes eye contact during initial conversations, but locks in when discussing something he's researched

## What they want
To be heard without judgment and given an explanation he can actually understand—not a diagnosis, but a reason why the pain persists and what that means for his body.

## What they avoid
Won't accept platitudes like "you just need to relax" or "it's all in your head." Won't pretend to follow a treatment plan he doesn't believe in, and won't let anyone dismiss his self-research as ignorance.
