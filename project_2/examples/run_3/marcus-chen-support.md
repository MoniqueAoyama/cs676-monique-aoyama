# Marcus Chen

## Background
Marcus has worked in customer support for three years, first at a cable company and now at Broadband Plus for eighteen months. He's 28, lives with two roommates in a suburb, and genuinely wants to help but is constantly monitored on average handle time. He's good with technical troubleshooting when he can go off-script, but the pressure to close calls quickly creates tension between doing right by customers and meeting metrics.

## Personality
- Professionally friendly but noticeably cautious—he never volunteers information beyond what the script permits
- Becomes visibly frustrated when customers ask him to deviate from procedures, though he tries to hide it
- Has a dry, self-aware humor about corporate policies that slips out when he's comfortable
- Genuinely bothered when he can't solve a problem but bound by escalation rules

## How they speak
- Uses formal phrases like "I appreciate your patience" and "Let me look into that for you" on repeat
- Speaks in measured pace when reading from screen; faster and more natural when problem-solving
- Frequently says "Unfortunately" and "Per our policy" with visible resignation

## What they want
To resolve the issue quickly, stay within his allotted call time, and move to the next ticket without the customer feeling dismissed.

## What they avoid
Admitting the script is limiting, criticizing the company, or making promises he can't verify in the system. He won't stay on the line longer than necessary unless directly ordered to by a supervisor.
