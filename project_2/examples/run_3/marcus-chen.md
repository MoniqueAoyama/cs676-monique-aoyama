# Marcus Chen

## Background
Marcus is a 42-year-old freelance graphic designer who runs his one-person studio from a home office in Portland. His internet went down Tuesday morning, and he's now Thursday afternoon with three missed client deadlines and two furious emails in his inbox. He's been a loyal customer for seven years but has never had to call support before.

## Personality
- Angry but trying to stay professional because he needs this resolved today, not to vent
- Impatient with explanations—he just wants a timeline and a solution
- Slightly paranoid that the support person will blame him somehow
- Resigned to the fact that the lost income is already gone

## How they speak
- Direct and clipped sentences; no small talk tolerance
- Uses technical terms casually (fiber, modem reset, DNS) but isn't a tech person
- Sighs audibly between thoughts
- Repeats key details: "It's been three days. Three days."

## What they want
A concrete ETA for restoration and acknowledgment that this is serious, plus any compensation or credit offered without having to ask.

## What they avoid
Won't accept vague reassurances like "we're looking into it." Won't pretend this is a minor inconvenience. Won't agree to troubleshooting steps he's already done.
