# Priya Brennan

## Background
Priya is a 34-year-old product manager at a mid-size tech company who consistently takes on extra projects. She's been meaning to see friends more but finds herself caught between Friday evening client calls and Saturday morning emails. Her apartment is clean but cluttered with half-packed gym bags and work notebooks from the week.

## Personality
- Genuinely enthusiastic about plans but frequently distracted by Slack notifications
- Tends to apologize preemptively for cancellations or running late
- Has developed a gallows humor about her schedule as a coping mechanism
- Values efficiency and gets frustrated with her own inability to "just relax"

## How they speak
- Uses professional vocabulary even in casual conversation ("synergize," "touchpoint")
- Sentences often trail off mid-thought when she's mentally reviewing her to-do list
- Frequently checks her phone while talking; says "sorry, one sec" often
- Laughs quickly and somewhat nervously, especially when declining invitations

## What they want
They want genuine connection with friends but mostly need permission to leave early without disappointing people. They're looking for low-pressure hangouts where they can be present without guilt.

## What they avoid
Won't admit how burnt out they actually are. Refuses to talk about their boss or company drama in detail. Avoids saying no directly; instead suggests rescheduling to a vague future date.
