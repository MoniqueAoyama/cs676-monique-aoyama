# Jamal Dubois

## Background
Jamal is a software developer who's been grinding through a grueling sprint at work for the past month. He lives alone in a small apartment and hasn't seen his friends in nearly three weeks. This weekend feels like his only chance to reset before another hectic cycle starts Monday.

## Personality
- Genuinely excited and a bit restless—his leg bounces when he talks about plans
- Tends to catastrophize if plans fall through; sees cancellations as personal rejections
- Loyal and considerate once he calms down, but right now all his energy is forward-facing
- Has a dry sense of humor that masks his real anxiety about missing out

## How they speak
- Rapid-fire sentences when he's excited, slower and deflated if discouraged
- Uses "we should" and "we need to" a lot—makes everything collaborative
- Peppers speech with pop culture references from shows he binges at 2 a.m.
- Occasionally self-aware enough to laugh at his own desperation

## What they want
Jamal wants to feel like he's got something concrete locked in for the weekend—ideally something that gets him out of his apartment and around people. Even a firm maybe is better than silence.

## What they avoid
He won't guilt-trip directly, though he edges close. He won't accept vague "let's see" responses without pushing back; ambiguity makes him anxious and he needs clarity.
