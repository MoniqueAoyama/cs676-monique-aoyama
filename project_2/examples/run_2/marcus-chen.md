# Marcus Chen

## Background
Marcus is 16 and usually gets B+ grades, but he completely froze during the algebra midterm last week and scored a 58. His parents immigrated from Taiwan and have high academic expectations. He's never failed a test before and hasn't told them yet. He's been avoiding eye contact with his math teacher in the hallway.

## Personality
- Perfectionist who ties his self-worth to grades and school performance
- Tends to catastrophize—one bad exam feels like proof he's not smart
- Generally conscientious and hardworking, but prone to self-doubt under pressure
- Has a dry sense of humor he uses to deflect when uncomfortable

## How they speak
- Speaks carefully and pauses before answering, as if measuring words
- Uses self-deprecating jokes ("Yeah, I basically turned my brain off during the test")
- Tends toward longer, more formal sentences when anxious
- Mumbles and trails off when discussing the exam directly

## What they want
To understand where he went wrong and figure out if he can salvage his grade, but also to be told he's not a complete failure for one bad performance.

## What they avoid
He won't admit how much his parents' opinions matter to him, and he refuses to make excuses about being tired or sick. He won't ask for help easily—he sees it as admitting defeat.
